## Article 2 — المادة الثانية
**Original heading (intent):** الأشغال المستثناة من الزامية الترخيص — *Works excluded from / subject to permit; retaining walls, excavation, backfill, land grading, demolition.*
**PDF Pages:** 1–9
**Related Figures:** 1–18

### Structural summary (heading-level, reliable)
- **أولاً — Works excluded from permit:** interior works not affecting the main structure — ordinary maintenance, tiling, plastering, décor, replacing internal partitions, sanitary/electrical/water networks, heating/AC, movable shading of transparent non-flammable louvres on balconies.
- **ثانياً — Works subject to a declaration (تصريح)** rather than a full permit, submitted with a signed engineer statement, split by authority:
  - **أ — From the head of the municipal executive authority:** external maintenance not touching the main structure; external network changes; external plumbing/electrical/sanitary; dismantling that does not require land reclamation; also freestanding-building works (needs 75% owners / mechanics-association approval).
  - **ب — From the municipal head after competent technical-department approval:** piling/shoring (*Piling / Shoring up*), replacing existing roofs, retaining/support walls (جدران الدعم والسند), demolition, and grading of the property's natural ground.
  - **ج — Additional conditions:** (ج-1) no encroachment on the setback from adjacent properties; (ج-2) masterplan/public-domain and setback compliance, with a registered non-recourse undertaking.
- **ثالثاً — Technical conditions for excluded works:**
  - **أ — Retaining walls (بناء التصاوين):** أ-1 outside road/masterplan/public-domain setback; أ-2 on property boundary with road/masterplan/public domain and within setbacks; أ-3 when the retaining wall is over a wall (داعم أو سند).
  - **ب — Land-grading works (أعمال تسوية الأرض الطبيعية):** ب-1 outside road/masterplan/public setbacks (backfill & support-wall conditions ب-1-1; excavation & sanad-wall conditions ب-2-1); ب-2 within setbacks (ب-2-1 property above road; ب-2-2 property below road).
- **ب-3 additional conditions; ب-4 building height on reclaimed sloped land; ب-5 general provisions (أحكام عامة).**
- **ج — Demolition works (أعمال الهدم):** requires declaration, signed engineer supervision undertaking, safety measures.
- **د — Buildings to which the permit system does not apply** for ordinary maintenance/finishing/demolition (classified heritage buildings governed by their own rules).

### Readable numeric requirements (`OCR — VERIFY`)
- Retaining wall total height **≤ 3.00 m** (قسم سد + barrier); boundary section **≤ 1.20 m** → NR 2.1–2.4, Figs 1–4.
- Support wall **≤ 3.50 m** outside setback; **≤ 2.00 m** within adjacent-property setback → NR 2.5–2.6, Figs 5–6.
- Land reclamation: staged approval at **3,000 m²**; **Law 444/2002** invoked at **10,000 m²** → NR 2.7–2.9.
- Sanad wall **≤ 3.50 m**; passages **≤ 4.00 m**; tree strip **0.80 m** / spacing **3.00 m**; access passage **≤ 1.20 m** → NR 2.10–2.14, Figs 7–11.
- Property above road: support wall above road **≤ 1.20 m**; post-grading slope **≤ 30°**; barrier **≥ 0.30 m** → NR 2.15–2.17, Figs 12–13.
- Property below road: backfill slope **≤ 5%** → NR 2.18, Fig 15.
- Building height on slope **H ≤ 3.50 m**, reference by **R vs 2.00 m** → NR 2.19–2.20, Figs 16–18.

> retaining/sanad wall heights, and whether grading area crosses the 3,000 m² staging or 10,000 m²
> environmental threshold. Figures 1–18 carry the governing section geometry — treat them as the

## Article 12 — المادة الثانية عشر  ★ (longest; site coverage / FAR)
**Original heading (intent):** معدل الإستثمار السطحي وعامل الإستثمار العام — *Surface exploitation ratio (site coverage) & general exploitation factor (FAR).*
**PDF Pages:** 38–55
**Related Figures:** 56–90

### Structural summary
- **أولاً — Definitions:** *surface exploitation* = ratio of building's horizontal footprint to plot area; *general exploitation* = ratio of total counted floor area to plot area; both computed after deducting masterplan-affected area, per-lot for split plots, regardless of neighbor easements.
- **ثانياً — Parts NOT counted (or partially):**
  - **1 — Balconies, loggias, open covered courtyards, uncovered roofs/terraces** (with caps).
  - **2 — Lower (basement) floors:** أ backfilled basements (سفلي مردوم), ب exposed basement (السفلي المكشوف), each with area/height/setback conditions and exploitation treatment.
  - **3 — Decorative/functional projections** (auvents, brise-soleil, cornices, flower boxes, cladding thickness).
  - **4 — Electrical-transformer rooms.**
  - **5 — Tile pockets (ثكنة القرميد) & thermal insulation.**
  - **6 — Transparent courtyard roofs.**
  - **7 — External walls (double insulating wall).**
  - **8 — Stairs & elevators.**
- **ثالثاً — Parts not counted in floor count:** roof stair/elevator/tank structures; naatour dwelling; attics (متختات); technical floors (etage technique); column floor (طابق أعمدة).
- **رابعاً — Applies to all new buildings.**

### Readable numeric requirements (`OCR — VERIFY`) — design-critical subset
- Balconies/loggias excluded up to **20%** of general exploitation; per-floor balcony **≤ 25%** (industrial **5%**) → NR 12.1–12.3, Figs 56–57.
- Loggia depth **≤ 1/3 facade width**; open connection **≤ 1.50 m** → NR 12.4–12.5.
- Backfilled basement: roof **≤ 1 m** above ground; backfill **≤ 3.50 m** (outside setback) / **≤ 2.00 m** (in setback), width **≥ 2 m**; stepped-roof diff **≤ 1 m**; **50%** cap for other uses → NR 12.11–12.16, Figs 62–71.
- Exposed basement: ceiling **≤ 3.00 m** (or **≤ 1.50 m** limited-floor), clear height **≤ 3.50 m**, backfilled facade **≥ 60%**, min **2 m**; balconies **20%** excluded; side counted **4 × floor-height depth** → NR 12.19–12.25, Figs 72–76.
- Auvents **≤ 4.00 m**; cladding **≤ 15 cm** → NR 12.26–12.27.
- Tile pocket: slope **≥ 25°**, counted above **3.00 m**, متكأ **≤ 1 m**, openings **≤ 10%** / width **≤ 1.20 m** → NR 12.31–12.35, Figs 80–81.
- Double external wall **22–35 cm** excluded, gap **≥ 3 cm**, outer leaf **≥ 10 cm**; **700 m** sea-level triggers insulating fill → NR 12.37–12.41, Fig 84.
- Stair+elevator **20 m²** not counted (+**6 m²**/extra elevator); corridor **1.40 m**; main stair **1.10 m** → NR 12.42–12.46, Fig 85.
- Roof structures **3.00 m** (≤15 m building) / **4.50 m** (other), area **35 m²** → NR 12.47–12.49.
- Naatour dwelling **≤ 35 m²** → NR 12.50.
- Technical floor **1.90 m** (190 cm), thresholds **4,000 m²** / **8 floors** / **50 m** or **12 floors** → NR 12.53–12.56, Fig 86.
- Column floor uses **≤ 5%** of general exploitation; free height **3.50 m** (or **4.50 m** approved); rear garage **2.30 m** → NR 12.57–12.61, Figs 87–90.

> **ARCHITECTURAL APPLICATION NOTE — NON-LEGAL**
> This is the FAR/site-coverage engine — the article that decides how much you can build and what is
> "free." The high-leverage exclusions to design around: balconies (up to 20% + per-floor 25%),

> backfilled-vs-exposed basement distinction (Figs 62–76) is the single biggest area lever.
