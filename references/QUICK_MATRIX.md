> **What this file is:** the lightweight first stop for single-value and routing lookups, so common
> questions do NOT load the 44 KB Master or 54 KB Ledger. Every value here is **copied from the
> existing verified references** (Master / Ledger) — nothing new was added, nothing reinterpreted.
>
> **Retrieval hierarchy:** `QUICK_MATRIX (this file) → Master Article block → Ledger Register`.
> Escalate to Tier 2/3 only when you need legal context, conditions/exceptions, exact figure
> geometry, or to confirm a value's context.
>
> **⚠️ Global caveats (apply to every row below):**
> - Every numeric value is **`OCR — VERIFY`** — the source Arabic OCR was corrupted; confirm against
>   the official Gazette (الجريدة الرسمية) of Law 646/2004 before any use.
> - **Values in this file are GENERAL Building-Law method/limits from the Application Decree.** They do
>   **NOT** include the plot's **BCR, FAR, maximum height, maximum floors, or setback distances** — those
>   are **zone/plot-specific** and set by the municipal Planning/Zoning sheet, **not** by the decree.
>   For any of those, return **`LOCAL ZONING VERIFICATION REQUIRED`** and see
>   `references/PLANNING_ZONING_WORKFLOW.md`.
